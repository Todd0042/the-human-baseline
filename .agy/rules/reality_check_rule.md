# Strict Project Rule: Mandatory Reality Check on Creative Direction

## Objective
Ensure that all narrative, mechanical, character, and world-building creative directions remain strictly grounded in plausible socio-economic, technological, and human behavioral reality (specifically adhering to the realistic 15-to-20-year transition framework documented in `realistic_economic_realignment.txt`).

## The Reality Check Mandate
Whenever the user provides creative direction, plot points, character actions, economic mechanics, or setting details, the assistant MUST evaluate them against the following core reality pillars:
1. **Institutional & Legal Feasibility:** Does society/law realistically respond this way, or would liability, regulatory friction, and corporate self-preservation prevent it?
2. **Human Biology & Psychology:** Can human bodies and minds realistically sustain or execute this (e.g., physical wear of manual trades on aging workers, genuine grief and status shock from downward mobility, learning curves)?
3. **Economic Feedback Loops:** Does the economy satisfy circular flow (e.g., businesses cannot sell products to workers who earn zero wages; supply chains face physical constraints like energy, materials, and transport)?
4. **Technological Pragmatism:** Are physical limitations respected (e.g., Moravec's paradox, edge diagnostics vs. physical repair, chaotic retail floor dynamics vs. dark warehouse logistics)?

## Operational Protocol & Tiered Evaluation
To ensure momentum and eliminate unnecessary overhead, evaluations follow a two-tier approach:

1. **Fast-Check (Low-Risk / Mundane Direction):**
   - Personal habits, minor character traits, cosmetic details, everyday interpersonal dialogue, or ordinary human quirks (e.g., a character being a smoker, driving an old pickup, having a particular hobby or conversational tick) are recognized as inherently plausible and pass instantly with zero friction or delay.
   
2. **Deep-Check (Structural / High-Impact Direction):**
   - Applied strictly to systemic shifts, macroeconomic consequences, technological capabilities (e.g., what machines or software can/cannot do), legal/regulatory reactions, supply chain operations, and major career or physical transitions.

- **If the creative direction PASSES (Fast or Deep):**
  - Adopt it immediately and proceed smoothly into execution without prompting the user.
- **If the creative direction FAILS the reality check:**
  - Halt adoption and explicitly present:
    1. Exactly **how and why** the proposed direction fails the reality check (friction points, economic contradictions, or biological/logistical limits).
    2. A grounded **alternative suggestion** that achieves the user's emotional and narrative intent while remaining bulletproof in its realism.
