# The Human Baseline: Master Narrative & Act Architecture

## Core Character Foundations
- **Todd (24 at start, mid-40s by finale):**
  - Lead diagnostic automotive technician / independent garage partner.
  - Sexy mid-length dark curly hair, sharp observant brown eyes, athletic build, grease-stained forearms, natural mechanical intuition.
  - Work ethic: Relentlessly persistent, grounded, hates corporate planned obsolescence, believes a well-maintained machine is honest.
- **Adriana (24 at start, mid-40s by finale):**
  - Retail department manager of a high-volume liquor annex connected to a major supermarket chain.
  - Dark observant eyes, sharp angular jawline, hair in a neat practical braid/ponytail, dry witty delivery, fiercely protective of her crew.
  - Work ethic: Meticulous organizer, handles high-stress chaotic storefront operations with calm authority, recognizes the human stories behind customer purchasing habits.

---

## The 9-Act Extended Narrative Arc

### Act I: The Warm Engine (Year 0) — "The Baseline"
- **Setting:** Todd’s bustling auto garage on an energetic Friday afternoon; Adriana’s lively, fully-staffed liquor annex prepping for weekend foot traffic; a loud, warm backyard dinner with close friends.
- **Atmosphere & Reality:** Life as it was before the subtle squeeze began. The economy feels predictable and optimistic. People complain about normal, mundane things: traffic, weekend plans, who's bringing what to the cookout.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *The Garage Floor:* Todd is in his element, bantering with his mentor-boss Pete while dialing in the timing belt on a clean late-model sedan. The bays are humming, invoices are paid without stress, and the air smells of honest motor oil and brake cleaner.
  - *The Retail Floor:* Adriana’s department is fully staffed with four energetic college clerks. She’s coaching a young cashier, laughing through the afternoon delivery rush, and building creative weekend display towers without corporate surveillance software breathing down her neck.
  - *The Backyard Table:* Todd, Adriana, Marcus (thriving at his junior tech job, excited about his upcoming promotion), and their circle share beers and grilled burgers as the sun sets. The future looks like a straight, upward line: career ladders, house savings, and standard 40-hour routines.

### Act II: The First Tremors (Years 1–2) — "Quiet Shifts"
- **Setting:** Todd’s garage bay on a Tuesday morning; Adriana’s stockroom receiving delivery.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *The Shift:* Adriana is forced to implement a new corporate Workforce Management algorithm that trims shift times by 8 minutes per worker per day, sparking quiet panic among her college-age clerks.
  - *The Warning Sign:* Marcus stops by the shop for a quick oil change, bragging about new corporate AI tools, unaware that his firm just initiated a silent hiring freeze for junior backfills.
  - *The Kitchen Table:* Todd and Adriana compare notes over dinner. Things feel normal on the surface, but the small efficiencies are starting to feel restrictive.

### Act III: The Severed Ladder (Years 3–4) — "Degrees of Separation"
- **Setting:** Sunday barbecue at Todd and Adriana’s house; local corporate office park.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *The Unraveling:* Marcus is laid off—not replaced by a robot, but because his three senior managers absorbed his entire workload using automated scaffolding. He cannot land an interview anywhere.
  - *The Retail Influx:* Adriana receives 40 applications for a single 16-hour-a-week cashier role, many from former paralegals, copywriters, and junior accountants.
  - *Todd’s Bay:* Todd encounters his first proprietary locked ECM on a commuter car. The customer is Marcus, who can't afford the $400 dealer software unlock fee. Todd finds an open-source OBD-II hardware workaround.

### Act IV: The Commuter Drain (Years 5–6) — "Idle Engines"
- **Setting:** Todd’s garage bays echoing with quiet; Adriana’s store during a Friday rush.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *Car Counts Drop:* As hybrid and remote white-collar work hardens and displaced workers stop driving, Todd's shop car count drops 25%. Preventative maintenance cycles stretch out.
  - *The Triage Customer:* A single mother working delivery gigs brings in a sedan with grinding brakes. Todd eats part of the labor cost to put safe pads on her car so she can keep earning.
  - *The Shrinkage Surge:* Adriana's store installs automated self-checkout lanes that backfire immediately. High-end bourbon and vodka theft spikes; corporate management blames her department instead of the flawed unstaffed system.

### Act V: The Middle-Age Squeeze (Years 7–9) — "Tolerances"
- **Setting:** Downtown streets showing commercial vacancies; Todd and Adriana’s living room surrounded by tax bills.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *Municipal Decay:* Downtown office vacancies cause a municipal commercial tax shortfall. City transit cuts routes, potholes multiply, and local property taxes jump 18% to balance the budget.
  - *The Retraining Illusion:* Marcus attempts to retrain as an electrician apprentice at age 31, throwing out his back within two months and facing the brutal reality that physical trades aren't an easy fallback.
  - *Todd’s Persistence:* Todd refuses to join a corporate chain franchise that requires proprietary vendor lock-in. He and his shop owner double down on independent diagnostics and older vehicle restoration, building a loyal cult clientele.

### Act VI: The Great Caregiving Crucible (Years 10–12) — "Generational Weight"
- **Setting:** Suburban nursing home; family living room converted into a makeshift care space.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *The Demographic Peak:* The massive Baby Boomer generation enters their 80s and 90s. Todd and Adriana’s aging parents face chronic health challenges.
  - *The Care Shortage:* Assisted living costs reach astronomical levels ($9,000/month) while understaffed facilities struggle. Todd and Adriana must balance full shifts with physical home caregiving.
  - *The Exhaustion Test:* Adriana coordinates family medications, lifting, and appointments with military precision; Todd rigs up ramps, handrails, and adaptive home hardware. Their persistence is tested to its absolute limit, yet their bond solidifies.

### Act VII: The Breaking Point & The Pushback (Years 13–15) — "Friction"
- **Setting:** Union hall / community center; logistics distribution hubs surrounded by picket lines.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *The National Strike Waves:* Warehouse workers and logistics drivers strike against punitive biometric pacing. News feeds report widespread public support for federal intervention.
  - *The Right-to-Repair Battle:* Todd travels to the state capitol with independent mechanics to testify against automaker software paywalls that prevent vehicle owners from fixing their own cars.
  - *The Turning of the Tide:* Public exhaustion with pure corporate optimization forces politicians to debate structural labor caps and compute taxation.

### Act VIII: The 32nd Hour (Years 16–18) — "The New Contract"
- **Setting:** Adriana’s newly restructured store schedule; Todd’s expanded three-bay independent garage.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *The Legislation Lands:* The federal 32-hour workweek law is enacted. Corporate payroll software must adapt: 4 days of work for 5 days of base pay.
  - *The Culture Shock:* Companies scramble to re-hire and spread hours. Adriana’s department expands its core headcount, allowing her to promote long-time part-timers into stable, dignified schedules.
  - *Apprentices in the Bay:* Todd finally takes on two young apprentices, teaching them hands-on diagnostic intuition, wiring repair, and oscilloscope analysis, rebuilding the lost generational bridge.

### Act IX: The Human Baseline (Years 19–20) — "Clocking Out"
- **Setting:** Back patio on a Friday afternoon (the new universal weekend); quiet sunset overlooking the neighborhood.
- **Pivotal Moments:**
  - *The Reflection:* Todd and Adriana, now seasoned veterans of their trades in their mid-40s, host a family and neighborhood gathering on a Friday that used to be a frantic workday.
  - *Marcus’s New Path:* Marcus has found steady ground managing municipal grid maintenance data—augmented by AI, but rooted in public infrastructure stability.
  - *The Hard-Won Peace:* They look out over their community. The world didn't end, and it didn't turn into a sterile utopia. It was a messy, painful transition, but by leaning on honest work, stubborn persistence, and human solidarity, they carved out a life with more time, deeper craft, and genuine dignity.
