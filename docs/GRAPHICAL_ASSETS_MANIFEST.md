# The Human Baseline: Graphical Assets Specification & Generation Prompts

This manifest identifies all character portraits and environmental background plates required for *The Human Baseline*.
All assets are authored in the grounded, cinematic 35mm film aesthetic (raw human skin texture, natural lens depth, realistic practical lighting, zero airbrushed CGI/plastic).

---
